# Music

Songs for the jukebox in the corner of every page. Drop MP3 or M4A files here and list them in
`src/features/music/tracks.ts` (title, artist); the jukebox stays hidden until the list has one
entry. Keep each file a few megabytes (128–192 kbps is plenty); they are fetched on demand, never
precached.

The site is public at its address, so only put here what you are comfortable sharing from your
own account: your own recordings, or songs you have the right to share. A short favourite passage
is safer than a whole album.
